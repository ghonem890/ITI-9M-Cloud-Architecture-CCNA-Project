Network Design

Topology, VLSM addressing plan and device inventory

Network Topology

 

The design has four logical zones: the HQ branch (10.10.0.0), the Regional Branch (10.20.0.0), the Edge / DMZ and the ISP / external network. Each branch has two routers in an HSRP pair and two switches joined by an EtherChannel.

Why a /29 toward the ISP? A /30 would use .1 and .2 for the two routers, colliding with the required public NAT address 50.50.50.2. A /29 keeps that address free and directly reachable.
